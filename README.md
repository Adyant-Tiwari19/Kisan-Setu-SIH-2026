## About Kisan Setu

Kisan Setu is a digital agricultural marketplace built to establish a direct connection between farmers and customers. It gives farmers a place to showcase their produce and gives retailers, households, and bulk buyers a clearer way to discover, compare, and purchase what they need.

The platform is built around a simple idea: the people who grow food should be able to reach the people who consume or sell it with fewer barriers in between.

## The problem with intermediaries

Traditional agricultural supply chains often pass produce through several layers of intermediaries before it reaches the customer. While intermediaries can play a role in distribution, a long and opaque chain can create serious challenges:

- Farmers may receive only a small share of the final selling price.
- Customers often pay more without knowing how the price was formed.
- Farmers have limited visibility into customer demand and market opportunities.
- Buyers may struggle to verify the source, freshness, quantity, and availability of produce.
- Information can be delayed or lost as produce moves through multiple hands.
- Price negotiation and coordination become slower and less transparent.
- Small and local farmers can find it difficult to reach larger or more reliable markets.

When the farmer and customer are disconnected, both sides lose visibility. Farmers lose opportunity, while customers lose confidence and choice.

## How Kisan Setu solves this

Kisan Setu reduces unnecessary distance between farmers and customers by creating a shared digital marketplace. Farmers can list their produce directly, and customers can discover available supply without depending entirely on a long chain of intermediaries.

### Direct farmer-to-customer connection

Farmers can present their crops, prices, available quantities, location, and other relevant details in one place. Customers can view this information, compare listings, and make more informed purchasing decisions.

### Better visibility for everyone

Kisan Setu makes important details easier to see, including:

- Produce type and origin
- Available quantity
- Price per unit
- Freshness and harvest information
- Distance from the buyer
- Seller and trust information

This visibility helps farmers communicate the value of their produce and helps customers understand what they are buying.

### More opportunity for farmers

Farmers can reach buyers beyond their immediate local network, manage their own listings, update inventory, and respond to demand more effectively. The platform gives them a stronger digital presence without requiring them to depend completely on intermediaries for market access.

### More confidence for customers

Customers, retailers, and bulk buyers can search for specific crops, compare available sellers, and choose produce using clear information. This supports a more confident and transparent buying experience.

### A stronger agricultural ecosystem

By improving communication between supply and demand, Kisan Setu supports a more connected food economy. The aim is not only to move produce faster, but to create fairer relationships between the people who grow food and the people who need it.

## What the platform offers

| For farmers | For customers and buyers |
| --- | --- |
| Create a farmer profile | Discover available produce |
| Publish produce listings | Search by crop and location |
| Manage quantity and pricing | Compare price, freshness, and distance |
| Update listing availability | Add produce to a cart and place orders |
| Reach retailers and bulk buyers | Source from farmers with greater confidence |

The platform also supports location-aware sourcing, order workflows, seller ranking, and demand forecasting to make agricultural trade more informed and efficient.

## Technology stack

Kisan Setu combines a modern web experience, a scalable API, and location-aware data services.

### Frontend

- **React** for the interactive user experience
- **TypeScript** for reliable application development
- **Vite** for a fast development and build workflow
- **Tailwind CSS** for responsive interface styling
- **React Router** for application navigation
- **i18next** for English and Hindi localization
- **Framer Motion** for purposeful interface animations

### Backend

- **Python** as the backend programming language
- **FastAPI** for the REST API and service layer
- **Pydantic** for data validation and API schemas
- **SQLAlchemy** for database interaction
- **JWT and OAuth2-compatible authentication** for protected access

### Data and intelligence

- **PostgreSQL** for reliable application data storage
- **PostGIS** for location-aware and spatial marketplace features
- **GeoAlchemy2** for spatial database integration
- **Scikit-learn and XGBoost** for forecasting and ranking capabilities
- **Pandas, NumPy, and Joblib** for data processing and model support

### Mobile and delivery

- **Capacitor** for Android application packaging
- Location, routing, and geocoding services to support sourcing and delivery workflows

## The vision

Kisan Setu is working toward a more direct, transparent, and connected agricultural marketplace where farmers have greater access to opportunity and customers have greater confidence in what they buy.
